Zion Tech Hub: Cohort Growth & Acquisition Intelligence

An end-to-end analysis of Cohort 9 and 10 registration data to guide ad spend for Cohort 11 and decide which regions to expand into.

1,218Total registrations
45Countries reached
7Courses offered
7Acquisition channels

The problem

Zion Tech Hub needed to know which marketing channels and regions actually drive sign-ups, so the Cohort 11 ad budget goes where it works.

What I did

  • Cleaned and modelled registration data from two cohorts
  • Built KPI cards, channel, course and country breakdowns
  • Added a monthly trend and a channel × course matrix
  • Added slicers so stakeholders can filter it themselves

Key findings

  • X (Twitter) drove 795 of 1,218 sign-ups (65%), far ahead of WhatsApp (205) and LinkedIn (167)
  • Nigeria accounts for 715 registrations; Ghana, South Africa and Kenya (100+ each) are the clearest expansion markets
  • Data Science & AI is the top course with 465 registrations
  • Sign-ups peaked in November 2025 (426) and January 2026 (435), then fell sharply

Global Life Expectancy at Birth (2000–2021)

A 21-year look at how long people live across countries and genders, and what that says about global health standards.

Questions asked

  • Which 10 countries have the highest life expectancy, and which 10 the lowest?
  • Which gender lives longer?
  • How did each gender change between 2000 and 2021, and what is the overall trend?

What I did

  • Removed redundant columns and cleaned the dataset
  • Summarised by country, gender and year in Excel
  • Built ranking, gender and trend charts
  • Presented the findings as a short report deck

Key findings

  • Japan leads at 83.16 years, reflecting strong healthcare, diet and environment
  • Sub-Saharan Africa has the lowest figures; Lesotho is at 48.09, Eswatini 48.35 and the Central African Republic 48.89
  • Women outlive men: females rose from 69.4 to 73.8 years, males from 64.6 to 68.9
  • Global longevity peaked in 2019, and the drop through 2021 likely reflects COVID-19

Analysing International Students' Mental Health

Does studying abroad affect mental health, and does the length of stay matter? I used SQL to test this on survey data from a Japanese international university.

-- Average scores for international students,
-- grouped by length of stay
SELECT stay,
       COUNT(*) AS count_int,
       ROUND(AVG(todep), 2) AS average_phq,
       ROUND(AVG(tosc), 2)  AS average_scs,
       ROUND(AVG(toas), 2)  AS average_as
FROM students
WHERE inter_dom = 'Inter'
GROUP BY stay
ORDER BY stay DESC;

The problem

A 2018 study found international students face higher mental-health risk, and that social connectedness and acculturative stress predict depression. I checked whether the data supports this and whether length of stay plays a part.

What I did

  • Explored the 286-row students table in PostgreSQL
  • Filtered to international students
  • Grouped by length of stay and averaged depression, social connectedness and acculturative stress scores

Key findings

  • Over the first four years, acculturative stress rises steadily, from 72.8 to 87.7
  • Depression scores edge up from 7.48 to around 9, while social connectedness dips slightly
  • Groups that stayed 5+ years are too small (1–3 students) for reliable conclusions

Hotel Booking Analysis

What drives bookings, cancellations and guest requests across a City Hotel and a Resort Hotel, and what the business can do about it.

3.43Avg nights per stay
101.83Avg daily rate (ADR)
409KTotal nights booked
12MTotal lead time (days)

The problem

The hotels needed to understand where guests come from, when and why bookings get cancelled, and which guests need the most attention, so they can protect revenue and plan staffing.

What I did

  • Cleaned the booking data and created a Total Nights column
  • Built KPI measures for stay length, ADR and lead time
  • Charted countries, cancellations, lead time, children and special requests
  • Added hotel and lead-time slicers

Key findings

  • Portugal is the core market with ~49K guests and the highest total ADR (4.5M)
  • August has the most cancellations, which follow the summer peak
  • Cancelled bookings carry more lead time (6.3M days) than completed stays (6.0M), so early bookings are a cancellation risk
  • Transient guests make most special requests (57K), and the City Hotel hosts 57% of children
